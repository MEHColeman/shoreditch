This site is the demo for [Shoreditch](https://github.com/MEHColeman/shoreditch),
a two-column Bridgetown theme for technical blogging.

Shoreditch is adapted from [Hyde](http://hyde.getpoole.com) by
[@mdo](https://github.com/mdo), and was also inspired by
[Flexible Jekyll](https://github.com/artemsheludko/flexible-jekyll) by Artem
Sheludko.

Everything here is placeholder content. The author's own blog is at
[mehcoleman.com](https://mehcoleman.com).
