[Bridgetown](https://www.bridgetownrb.com) is a _Jamstack_ alternative to
Jekyll.<!--more--> The author, Jared White, was concerned about the stagnation
of Jekyll's development and created a modern successor. Both are excellent
static site generators, and are well worth checking out.
